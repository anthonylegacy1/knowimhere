# I’m Here Card Visual

## Goal
Make the existing orange private-participation card explain the feature at a glance without changing its approved copy or action.

## Changes
- Add a warm, realistic phone-in-hand visual beneath the description.

- Keep the current orange card, headline, description, button, spacing language, and mobile stacking.
- Fit the visual inside a rounded, accessible frame so it supports rather than overwhelms the copy.
