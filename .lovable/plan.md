# Know I’m Here Homepage Refinement

## Goal
Refine the existing homepage into one polished Detroit-centered product while preserving all routes, resident tools, Demo Mode, Ask KIH, recommendations, transportation, neighborhood routing, check-in, partner dashboard, privacy content, and existing resource logic.

## Homepage flow
Reorder the existing homepage into this narrative:

1. Detroit skyline hero
2. Overlapping phone walkthrough
3. Core connection statement and five-step journey
4. Personalized discovery and “For You” entry points
5. Ask KIH entry point
6. Multi-generational examples and opportunity hubs
7. Help Me Get There
8. Private “I’m Here” check-in
9. My Neighborhood and local participation
10. Everyday Connect digital confidence
11. How We Got Here: Fast Freddy → Everyday Connect → Know I’m Here
12. Partner impact preview using clearly labeled Buildathon demonstration data
13. Discover Detroit landmark gallery
14. Privacy, responsible AI, final message, and existing footer

Existing supporting sections such as the connection gap, intelligent front door, and resident priorities will remain, but will be placed where they support this story instead of interrupting it.
